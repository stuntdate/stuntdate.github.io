Questions

What is STUNTDATE?

A 24-hour, humans-only gig platform. Everyday people create out-of-the-ordinary paid gigs for each other — in real life or over FaceTime — and get paid the same day the gig is finished.

How does it work?

Someone creates a gig and names their price. A real human takes it, shows up (or FaceTimes in), does the thing, and gets paid the same day.

How many gigs can I do?

As many as you want. Wake up, do a morning gig, head to an afternoon gig, keep going. Good reviews help you get picked for more gigs.

What kind of gigs?

Anything a human can do for another human. Come with me to a funeral so I'm not alone. Throw a pie in my friend's face. Teach me mahjong on FaceTime. Be one of 33 Santa's elves at a Christmas fair. Cook with a nonna in Italy to the sounds of Sinatra.

Is it a dating app?

No. It's gigs. You're hiring a human — or getting paid to be one.

Why humans only?

Because the world is not our algorithm. No bots take gigs, and no AI does the work. Humans only. AI don't apply.

Who can join?

Anyone 18 or older during the beta. Humans only.

When can I use it?

We're in beta and opening city by city. Get on the list, add your city, and we'll reach out when gigs go live near you. FaceTime gigs don't wait for your city: they work from anywhere, from day one. Get your virtual car driven all over NYC, baby.
