1. Who can use STUNTDATE

You must be at least 18 years old. You agree to give true information and to use contact details that reach you.

2. What STUNTDATE is (and isn't)

STUNTDATE is a website where people create gigs and other people offer to do them, in person or over FaceTime. Right now it's a beta trial. We are not an employer, agency, or party to any gig. People who create gigs and people who do them are independent of us and of each other. Any deal about a gig, including what, when, where and how much, is only between the gig creator and the Stuntdate.

This is just the trial phase. When the full app launches, we plan to play a bigger part in guiding your safety.

3. Payments

During the beta, gig creators and Stuntdates pay each other directly, using whatever method they agree on. STUNTDATE does not process, hold, guarantee, or refund any payment, and doesn't take a fee during the beta. STUNTDATE makes zero dollars from this trial. Payment problems are between the people involved, but please tell us so we can keep the community honest.

4. Keep it legal

Nothing illegal is permitted here. That includes violence and the sale of drugs, sex, or people. It's all a no-no. If you need more info on what's illegal, ask one of us or your favorite AI. We can remove any gig or person, at any time, for any reason.

5. Our heartfelt advice and guidance

You're responsible for your own safety, but we'd like to give you some advice and guidance. For in-person gigs, meet somewhere public first, tell a friend where you're going, and have your own way home. Before you meet, get a picture of the other person's ID or socials. When you meet, take a picture of them and send it to a friend or family member right away. Drop a pin and let someone follow your location. Either person can end a gig if it feels wrong. In an emergency, call 911 first, then please reach out to us.

Questions or problems: stuntdate@gmail.com. We want you happy.
