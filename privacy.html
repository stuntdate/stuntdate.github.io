Privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Short version: we only keep what you type into the beta form, we use it to tell you when STUNTDATE launches, and we never sell it. Email us and we'll delete it.

What we collect

When you join the beta list:

  • your email
  • your city, if you add it
  • whether you'd create gigs, take gigs, or both
  • if you'd create gigs: the gig you named and what you'd pay

When anyone visits the site, we count the visit: which page, the site that sent you (just its name, like instagram.com), any campaign tag in the link, and whether you're on a phone or a computer. No cookies, no tracking across other sites, and we don't store your IP address.

Why

  • to email you about the STUNTDATE beta and launch
  • to figure out which cities and gigs to start with
  • to see which links bring people in

Who else sees it

Nobody we sell to — we don't sell or rent your info, ever. It's stored with Supabase (our database) and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs for security.

How long we keep it

Until STUNTDATE launches and for as long as you want updates. Ask us any time and we'll delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your info, or to stop getting emails. If you're in California, that includes your rights under the CCPA/CPRA; if you're in the UK or EU, your rights under GDPR. We'll answer within 30 days.
