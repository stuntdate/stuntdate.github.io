Privacy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Short version: we only keep what you give us, we use it to run STUNTDATE, and we never sell it. Your contact details are never shown on the site. They're only passed to someone when you've both agreed to a gig. Email us and we'll delete your info.

What we collect

When you join the beta list

  • your email
  • your city, if you add it
  • whether you'd create gigs, take gigs, or both
  • if you'd create gigs: your gig idea, price, FaceTime or in real life, and whether it's OK to show it on the site
  • that you confirmed you're 18 or older
  • whether you asked for the weekly "latest gigs" email
  • the random line on your Stuntdate card

During the beta trial

  • Gigs you create: the gig, price, city, timing, FaceTime or in real life, and how to reach you
  • Gigs you offer to do: your name or nickname, how to reach you, and any note you add
  • "Gig done" reports: whether you were paid, which payment app, your thumbs up or down, and anything you write. If you email us a payment screenshot, we keep it with your report.
  • Feedback you send us, with your name only if you add it

When anyone visits

We count visits: which page, the site that sent you (just its name, like instagram.com), any campaign tag in the link, and whether you're on a phone or a computer. We count which gigs get tapped and shared, never who tapped or shared them or who they sent them to. No cookies, no tracking across other sites, and we don't store your IP address.

Why

  • to reach out about the beta and launch
  • to show gigs on the gig board and book them
  • to keep people safe and remove anyone who breaks the rules
  • to figure out which cities and gigs to start with, and what's working
  • to see which links bring people in

Who sees what

On the site: gigs show without your name, email, or contact details. If you tick "OK to show my gig," we may show your gig and price as an example on stuntdate.com or our social media, still without your name.

The other person in a gig: once a gig is booked, we pass each person's contact details to the other so you can sort out time, place and payment. Only then, and only for that gig.

Our services: your info is stored with Supabase (our database), a copy of each signup is emailed to our team inbox using Resend and Gmail, and the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs for security.

Emails: we only email you the latest gigs, about once a week at most, if you ticked the box. Every one has an unsubscribe link. We don't email you for anything else, except about a gig you created or are part of. Those emails are sent through Resend.

Nobody else. We don't sell or rent your info, and we don't share it for advertising, ever. We'd only hand it over if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Payments: we never see or store your bank or card details. Payments happen in whatever app you and the other person use, under that app's own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

For as long as you're part of STUNTDATE or want updates. Gig and safety reports may be kept longer if we need them to resolve a problem. Ask us any time and we'll delete your info.

Keeping it safe

Only the STUNTDATE team can see the signup list and gig details. No system is perfect, so please don't send us anything you wouldn't want stored, like ID numbers or bank details.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your info, or to stop hearing from us. If you're in California, that includes your rights under the CCPA/CPRA. If you're in the UK or EU, it includes your rights under GDPR. We'll answer within 30 days.

Kids

STUNTDATE is for adults 18+. We don't knowingly collect info from anyone younger, and we'll delete it if we find out.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes, we'll tell you.
